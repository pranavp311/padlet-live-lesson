Sections

00:00

Plants as producers

Plants make their own food. Energy from the sun passes to plants, then to the animals that eat them.

04:30

Photosynthesis

Chlorophyll in the chloroplasts captures energy from sunlight. With it, the plant turns water and carbon dioxide into glucose, which it uses to grow, and oxygen, which it releases into the air.

10:05

Glucose

Glucose is C₆H₁₂O₆. In water it closes into a ring of five carbons and one oxygen. Four ring carbons carry an OH group; carbon 6 sits outside the ring with its own OH.

12:05

Graphing a quadratic

y = x² − 2x + 1 factors as (x − 1)². It crosses the y-axis at (0, 1) and touches the x-axis once, at its vertex (1, 0). The curve is symmetric about x = 1.

Key terms

Chlorophyll
Green pigment that captures light energy.
Chloroplast
Part of a plant cell where photosynthesis happens.
Glucose
Sugar the plant makes and uses to grow.
Hydroxyl group
An OH group bonded to a carbon atom.
Vertex
Turning point of a parabola.
Axis of symmetry
Vertical line through the vertex that splits the curve into mirror halves.
